Sources

sourcestateassessedfunded bitsmin-entropyperfect at nShannonsamples nlast window min / ShannonMarkovRCT worstAPT worst

Entropy columns are bits per sample, 8 at most, measured on the raw samples over everything collected since the last reset. assessed is what the release budget counts. funded bits is how many bits of each source's credit were spent on released blocks since the last reset, and its share of the total: exactly who paid for the output. Every block costs 256. Credit thrown away by a health failure, an assessment change or a DUMP never counts. min-entropy is the NIST most-common-value estimate, red when it falls below the assessment. It carries a 99% safety margin that only shrinks with more samples, so compare it with perfect at n: what an ideal random source reads at the same sample count. A perfect source reaches 7.9 at about 1.8 million samples (30 minutes for a 1 kHz source, 4 for the hardware RNG). Shannon is the average. Markov is the dependency estimate over 128-bit paths. RCT and APT are the NIST continuous tests: worst repeat run and worst count in a 512-sample window since the last health reset, against the cutoff that fails the source, failures after the dot. H healthy, D degraded, W warming, F failed and latched. mix rows never fund a block. Swipe the table sideways for more columns.

Motion tuning

To tune: rest the guitar still and watch the Movement trace. Both lines should stay under the dashed line; if one does not, raise that trigger one step. Then play and move: strums and body movement should cross the line. Lower a trigger if movement you want counted stays under. For micro movement, rotation is the sensitive one: 1 to 3 °/s picks up a lean or a breath, while acceleration bottoms out around 2 or 3 steps (16 to 24 mg), where the sensor's own noise begins.

Assessment

Enter Phase 0 results here. Changing an assessment discards unspent credit. An assessment above zero makes a source count toward releases; only the MPU sources, clock_beat, hw_rng and adc_noise can count. The rest are mixed in but can be steered from outside, so they never count.

Default 4, which needs the MPU. hw_rng is credited too, so 1 releases blocks from the ESP32's hardware RNG alone (true random while the radio is on) when the MPU is missing; the MPU sources join automatically when it answers. 2 also counts adc_noise once you give it an assessment.
